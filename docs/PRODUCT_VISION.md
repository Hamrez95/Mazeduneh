# Product vision

## Promise

Mazeduneh (مزه‌دونه) helps customers discover and buy fresh nuts, dried fruit, handmade fruit leather, lower-sugar homemade sweets, protein snacks, and gifts in a fast, calm, mobile-first journey.

## Primary outcomes

- Customer completes a first purchase in under three minutes.
- Product freshness, grade, origin, weight, allergens, and storage guidance are clear before payment.
- Owner can run orders, catalog, stock, promotions, and reports from a mobile admin app.
- Every stock movement and sensitive admin action is auditable.

## Initial customer segments

1. Household buyers seeking fresh daily products, including nuts and homemade snacks.
2. Gift buyers for Nowruz, Yalda, Ramadan, birthdays, and celebrations.
3. Corporate buyers requiring branded bulk gift packs.
4. Health-conscious customers seeking low-salt nuts, no-added-sugar dried fruit, and portioned protein snacks.

## MVP scope

- Product catalog, categories, variants by weight, search, filters, product pages.
- Cart, coupon, address, shipping quote, payment handoff, order confirmation, tracking.
- Customer account and guest checkout.
- Admin dashboard, orders, catalog, pricing, inventory ledger, low-stock alerts.
- Basic sales and inventory reports.
- Responsive storefront and installable admin PWA/Android app.

## Explicit non-goals for MVP

- Marketplace / multi-vendor operation.
- Microservices or Kubernetes.
- Full accounting ERP.
- Native customer mobile app.
- AI recommendations before reliable behavioral data exists.
