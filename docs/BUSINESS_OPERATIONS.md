# Business and operations launch plan

## Stage 0 — validation and legal readiness

- Confirm brand/domain/social handles and start trademark review.
- Define legal seller identity, invoice rules, return policy, privacy policy and terms.
- Select initial 20–40 SKUs instead of launching an oversized catalog.
- Approve suppliers and record origin, grade, purchase unit, minimum order and lead time.
- Test packaging for freshness, breakage, moisture, labeling and courier handling.
- Define launch geography, shipping promise and customer support hours.

## Stage 1 — operational model

### Product master data

Every sellable variant requires SKU, barcode if applicable, net weight, package type, sale price, purchase cost, tax treatment, origin, grade, allergens, storage instructions, shelf life, low-stock threshold and publish status.

### Batch and stock control

- Receive inventory by supplier lot/batch.
- Record received weight/quantity, purchase cost, packing date and expiry/best-before.
- Use FIFO/FEFO where shelf life requires it.
- Record immutable movements for receiving, reservation, sale, release, return, waste and adjustment.
- Investigate all manual adjustments and require a reason.

### Order fulfillment

Paid → picking → quality check → packing → label/tracking → handoff → delivered.

Define cut-off times, packaging checklist, substitution policy, damaged-order handling and evidence for handoff.

## Stage 2 — commercial launch

- Soft-launch to a controlled audience before paid acquisition.
- Measure conversion, failed payment rate, picking errors, delivery delays, refund rate and repeat purchase.
- Build initial campaigns around daily mixes, gifting and seasonal occasions.
- Collect verified reviews only after delivery.
- Add corporate ordering with quotation and lead-time rules after consumer fulfillment is reliable.

## Accounting minimum

- Daily payment-gateway reconciliation against paid orders.
- Separate revenue, shipping income, discount, refund, cost of goods and packaging cost.
- Store purchase cost per batch to estimate gross margin accurately.
- Prevent silent edits to finalized financial records; use reversal/adjustment entries.

## Launch KPIs

- Storefront conversion rate
- Checkout completion rate
- Payment verification failure rate
- Average order value
- Gross margin after discount, packaging and payment fees
- On-time dispatch rate
- Picking/packing error rate
- Stock variance and waste rate
- Refund/complaint rate
- 30/60/90-day repeat purchase rate

## Operational release gate

Do not advertise broadly until at least ten complete test orders and a controlled batch of real orders have passed payment, reservation, picking, packing, shipping, delivery, refund and reconciliation checks.
