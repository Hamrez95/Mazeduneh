# Mazeduneh packaging artwork

This catalog slice keeps the previously published transparent artwork and adds the seven supplied packaging scenes as separate WebP assets.

| Asset | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `dragon-box-new.webp` | Protein/cocoa cookie packaging and hero depth |
| `pistachio-pouch-new.webp` | Pistachio product and hero foreground |
| `almond-pouch-new.webp` | Almond product and hero side layer |
| `walnut-character-new.webp` | Walnut product and hero accent |
| `pistachio-scene-new.webp` | Pistachio detail-gallery/lifestyle view |
| `gift-boxes-new.webp` | Mixed nuts, dried fruit and gift packaging |
| `cookie-boxes-new.webp` | Oat/protein cookie packaging and gallery |

The supplied black and cream backgrounds were removed from the isolated packaging scenes and exported with an alpha channel. The two lifestyle scenes remain available as gallery imagery so the storefront can show both a clean product cutout and a richer brand context.

Product detail pages now expose the mapped artwork as an accessible thumbnail gallery. The public demo hero uses a restrained floating motion layer and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
